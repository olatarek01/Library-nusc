# نظام مكتبة جامعة مدينة السادات الأهلية

An Arabic, right-to-left library visit system: students scan a QR code and record their visit, while library staff manage books and review activity from a private dashboard.

## Look and feel

- Full RTL layout, Cairo font everywhere.
- Deep teal primary (#0d5c63 / #084c61), olive-green accents, off-white page background (#f8fafc), white cards, dark charcoal text.
- Mobile-first: the student form is designed for phones, the dashboard adapts from phone to desktop.
- Header branding: "جامعة مدينة السادات الأهلية" / "نظام تسجيل ومتابعة استخدام المكتبة".

## Student flow (the QR page)

Public page at `/library-visit`; the site root redirects there.

- Welcome text: "مرحبًا بك في مكتبة جامعة مدينة السادات الأهلية" and "يرجى تسجيل بيانات زيارتك للمكتبة".
- Fields: student name, student ID (validated), faculty (from the database), book (searchable, only active books of the chosen faculty).
- Date and time recorded automatically in Egypt time.
- Button disables while saving, Arabic loading/validation messages, repeat submissions blocked.
- Success screen: summary card with name, faculty, book, date, time, plus an option to record another visit.

## Staff area

Sign-in with email and password at `/admin/login`; everything under `/admin` is private.

Sidebar: الرئيسية، الزيارات، الكتب، الكليات، التقارير، رمز الاستجابة، تسجيل الخروج.

- **الرئيسية** — live counters (total visits, today, this month, total books, registered students) plus charts for visits over time, visits per faculty, most-read books, and written insights (peak hours, most active faculties, rarely used books).
- **الكتب** — searchable table with faculty filter, add/edit forms (title, author, faculty, category, code, status) and deactivate with an Arabic confirmation dialog.
- **الزيارات** — paginated log with search by student name/ID, faculty and date filters, sorting.
- **الكليات** — list of the seven faculties with their book and visit counts.
- **التقارير** — date range and faculty filters, summary figures, and CSV export with Arabic headers that open correctly in Excel.
- **رمز الاستجابة** — generated QR code for the student page, copy link, and a printable high-resolution poster.

## Data

Lovable Cloud is enabled for the database and staff sign-in.

- Faculties seeded with exactly the seven listed colleges — no fake students or visits.
- Books linked to a faculty, with category, code, active flag.
- Visits store student name and ID, faculty, book, date and time.
- The database rejects a visit whose book does not belong to the chosen faculty.
- Anyone can record a visit and read active faculties/books; only signed-in staff can manage books, faculties and read the full visit log.

## Technical notes

- TanStack Start routes: `/` (redirect), `/library-visit`, `/admin/login`, and `/admin/*` under an authenticated layout.
- Supabase migration creates `faculties`, `books`, `library_visits` with grants, RLS policies, a trigger enforcing book/faculty match, and the seven faculty rows.
- Reads and writes go through typed server functions; staff routes use the authenticated middleware.
- Charts via Recharts, QR via the `qrcode` package rendered to a high-resolution canvas/SVG.
- Egypt timezone stamping computed server-side (Africa/Cairo).
- Design tokens added to `src/styles.css`; Cairo loaded via a `<link>` in the root route.
